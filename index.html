About

I’m a biomedical engineer and AI researcher in the BioImage Research Lab (BIRL), Department of Biomedical Engineering, Islamic University, Kushtia, where I’m an M.Sc. student.

My M.Sc. thesis builds a physics-guided conditional diffusion framework that recovers high-fidelity, uncertainty-aware ultrasound images from sparse plane-wave RF data. I want to extend this line of work to MRI and CT, with methods that are accurate, trustworthy and efficient enough for real-time, resource-constrained clinical use.

I’m seeking a fully funded PhD in physics-informed generative models for medical image reconstruction. My training includes clinical exposure in radiology, ultrasound and nuclear medicine.

Research

Alongside my thesis, I study deep learning for diagnosis from medical images, using CNN, transformer and attention-based architectures with explainable AI for brain tumor, stroke, Alzheimer’s and breast cancer detection and classification.
